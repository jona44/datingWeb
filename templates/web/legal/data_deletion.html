Delete Directly From the App / Website

If you are logged in, you can delete your account immediately with real-time confirmation:

Request Deletion via Web / Email (Without Logging In)

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your account, you can request that our compliance team manually purge your account and all associated data:

Send an email with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - {{ brand_name }}" to:

support@lovebyte.online

Please include the phone number or email address associated with your account so we can verify and fulfill your request. Manual requests are processed within 48 to 72 hours.

What Data is Deleted?

🗑️ Permanently Deleted Immediately:

  • Profile data (name, bio, age, location, preferences)
  • All uploaded profile photos and media files
  • Matches, likes, skips, and mutual interactions
  • Chat messages and conversation history
  • Push notification tokens & device credentials

📋 Data Retention Policy:

  • We do not retain personal identifying records after deletion.
  • Security logs may retain anonymized event timestamps for up to 30 days to prevent fraud and comply with statutory requirements, after which they are permanently overwritten.
Important Note: Deleting your account is permanent. Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered or restored.
